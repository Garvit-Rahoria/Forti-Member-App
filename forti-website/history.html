Our Rich Legacy

FORTI was registered in 1965 as Rajasthan Vayapar Udyog Mandal by former members of the Rajasthan Chamber of Commerce — JP Bajaj, Umrao Mal Chordia, Vishwanatha Kejriwal, and Shri Periwal with the motive of supporting businesses and trade within the state, facilitating conversations that breed change and innovation to bring about a bigger change in the business world.

On 7th February 1980, Rajasthan Vayapar Udyog Mandal was re-named "Federation of Rajasthan Trade and Industry" with the same mission and vision at the core of its foundation. Umra Amatal Chordia became the first elected President of FORTI, remained in power from 1995-1998, and contributed significantly to the growth of FORTI.

Post that, FORTI has been headed by incredible visionaries who have been the guiding light of the organization, helping it achieve new milestones. Currently, FORTI is headed by Mr. Suresh Agarwal who is the only FORTI President to have been re-elected to power for 4 consecutive terms.

Under the guidance of these exceptional leaders, FORTI continues on its stride towards ease of conducting business and trade and helping businessmen establish their presence globally. FORTI is defined not only by its presence and people but it is also defined by its fundamental values that are reflected through its daily activities and decisions. These fundamental values are what propel FORTI to new heights.

"The key to a successful business is making the most of resources available at your disposal while exploring new opportunities of expansion and networking. Mr. I. C. Agarwal is a successful industrialist, a distinguished educationalist, and a social philanthropist. Mr. Agarwal completed his B.Tech.
