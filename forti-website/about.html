About FORTI

Learn about the apex chamber of Trade & Industry in Rajasthan — our purpose, vision, mission and what drives us forward.

Federation of Rajasthan Trade and Industry, abbreviated as FORTI, is a registered body with the Registrar of Non-Trading Companies and is the apex chamber of Trade and Industry in Rajasthan. FORTI acts as a bridge between the Government of India at the National Level and the Government of Rajasthan at the state level for its members. It carries out its responsibilities as an intermediary between the government and its members for policy advocacy and dissemination of information.

FORTI intends to make cohesive, streamlined, and systematic efforts to enhance international business in identified countries with favorable business opportunities and more significant synergies regarding the business environment, development needs, potential markets, and development needs in a liberalizing economy.
To facilitate discussions and seminars to enhance international business in identified countries with favorable business opportunities, and more significant synergies regarding the business environment, development needs, potential markets, and development needs in a liberalizing economy. FORTI works to bridge the gap between industry needs and government policy to create a conducive environment for all businesses in Rajasthan.
To enhance the efficiency and global competitiveness of the Indian industry and expand business opportunities in domestic and foreign markets through a range of specialised services and international linkages while also ensuring the businesses have the right to business ethics.
FORTI's mission is to promote the culture and business of Rajasthan globally by collaborating with partners, hosting exhibitions, providing employment opportunities to women, creating favorable policies, establishing Jaipur Night Bazaars, organizing award shows, and providing a platform for start-ups.
What We Do

Ensuring the Ease of Business and Trade

FORTI bridges the gap between government, industry, and international markets — making business simpler and stronger for all members.

01

Facilitates Discussions

FORTI regularly hosts conversations and facilitates discussions between the government, industries, and traders, ensuring the ease of business in accordance with government rules, laws, and regulations.

02

Hosts Seminars

FORTI also hosts seminars periodically to hold discussions with businessmen, industrialists, and traders, discussing their plans and needs to build a stronger business community.

03

Strong International Network

FORTI's presence is not only limited to India, and now has 8 international branches, helping Indian traders and businessmen in foreign countries leverage a strong global network.
