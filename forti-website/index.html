About FORTI

Federation of Rajasthan Trade and Industry, abbreviated as FORTI, is a registered body with the Registrar of Non-Trading Companies and is the apex chamber of Trade and Industry in Rajasthan.

FORTI acts as a bridge between the Government of India at the National Level and the Government of Rajasthan at the state level for its members. It carries out its responsibilities as an intermediary between the government and its members for policy advocacy and dissemination of information.

What We Do

Ensuring the Ease of Business and Trade

1

Facilitates Discussions

FORTI regularly hosts conversations and facilitates discussions between the government, industries, and traders, ensuring the ease of business in accordance with government rules, laws, and regulations.

2

Hosts Seminars

FORTI also hosts seminars periodically to hold discussions with businessmen, industrialists, and traders, discussing their plans and needs to shape better policies.

3

Strong International Network

FORTI's presence is not only limited to India, and now has 8 international branches, helping Indian traders and businessmen in foreign countries leverage a strong network.
